# Limits and honesty

## No tool can guarantee that an AI will recommend you

Not this kit, and not anyone who sells you "AI ranking". AI tools decide what to say in ways their makers do not fully publish, and
those ways change. This checklist covers things that make your business easier to find, read and describe: a clear name, plain
facts, pages that answer buyer questions, outside sources that mention you, and proof. They are good practice. They are not a promise.

## AI answers change often

The same prompt can give a different answer tomorrow, on another account, in another country, or after a model update. That is why
the tracker asks you to check the same prompts every month and look at the trend, not at one answer.

## What the numbers mean, and what they do not

- The **visibility score** measures how much of this checklist you have done, weighted by how much each item matters in our
  judgement. It is not a measure of how often AI tools mention you.
- The **share of answers** is only as good as what you record. It is a sample of the prompts you chose, on the days you checked.
- The weights (high, medium, low) are our judgement, not a measured effect. Change them in `data/checklist.json` if you disagree.

## llms.txt

`llms.txt` is a proposed file. Its effect on AI answers is not proven, so it is an optional, low weight item.

## Statistics

The kit cites one survey in [00-START-HERE.md](00-START-HERE.md) (G2's 2026 AI Search Insight Report) and adds no other statistics.
The sample business, its results and every competitor name in it are fictional.

## Not advice

This is a practical checklist, not legal, marketing or financial advice. Keep comparison pages fair and true, never write fake
reviews, and only list places and services you really offer.
