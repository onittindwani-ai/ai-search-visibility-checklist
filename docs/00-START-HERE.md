# Start here

## Why this kit exists

More buyers now ask an AI tool before they search Google. In G2's 2026 AI Search Insight Report, a survey of 1,076 B2B software
buyers, 51% said they start their research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google
([G2, The Answer Economy, 2026](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report)).

So the question for your business is simple: when a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude or Google AI Overviews for
someone like you, are you named? This kit helps you check that, and fix what is in your control.

## The fastest path (about 30 minutes)

1. Open the app: double click `app/index.html`, or run `python3 tools/serve.py` and go to http://localhost:8767.
2. Look around the fictional sample, then click **Start my own**.
3. Answer the 39 checklist items honestly. Yes only when it is really done.
4. Do the top 3 fixes the app lists first.
5. In the AI answer tracker, add 10 to 20 buyer prompts from the library, ask each one on the 5 platforms, and record what you see.
6. Repeat the tracker check once a month. Watch your share of answers and who else is named.
